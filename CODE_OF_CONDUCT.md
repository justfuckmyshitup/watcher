# Code of Conduct

Watcher should be a practical, respectful project for people exploring local-first AI workflows.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but be precise about risks.
- Protect privacy when sharing logs, screenshots, notes, and examples.
- Avoid posting real secrets, private data, or sensitive screen content.
- Keep discussions focused on making the project safer, clearer, and more useful.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, sharing private data without consent, or encouraging hidden capture/surveillance use cases.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that undermine the privacy and safety goals of the project.
